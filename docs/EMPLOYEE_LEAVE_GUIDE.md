# Employee Leave Guide 🏝️

This guide explains how leave works at our company and how to use **LeaveHub**, the leave application.
It applies to all current and new employees.

> **Items marked ⚠️ HR TO CONFIRM** are company policy decisions that the app does not enforce.
> HR should fill these in before sharing this document.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. Your **admin** creates your account and gives you your email and a temporary password.
2. Open LeaveHub in your browser and sign in.
3. You land on **My Leave**, where you can see your balances, apply for leave, and track your requests.

| Tab | Who sees it | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 🏠 My Leave | Everyone | Balances, apply for leave, request history |
| ✅ Approvals | Managers, Admins | Review leave requests from others |
| 📅 Team Calendar | Everyone | See who is off and when |
| 👥 Users | Admins | Add new employees |

---

## 2. Leave types and yearly allowance

Every employee gets the following **per calendar year** (1 January to 31 December):

| Leave type | Days per year | Paid? | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏖️ Annual | 20 | Paid | Holidays, trips, personal time off |
| 🤒 Sick | 10 | Paid | Illness or medical appointments |
| ☕ Casual | 5 | Paid | Short-notice personal matters |
| 💤 Unpaid | 30 | **Unpaid** | When your paid leave is used up, or for extended time off |

- Each type has its **own** balance. Using Sick leave does not reduce your Annual leave.
- Your remaining days are always shown on the **My Leave** page.
- ⚠️ **HR TO CONFIRM:** Whether Annual/Sick/Casual are fully paid, and whether unused days carry over to next year. By default the app **resets balances each year with no carry-over**.

---

## 3. How days are counted

- Only **weekdays (Monday to Friday)** are counted. Saturdays and Sundays are never deducted.
- **Public holidays are not excluded automatically.** If a public holiday falls inside your leave dates, it is still counted as a leave day. ⚠️ **HR TO CONFIRM** how holidays should be handled.
- Both the **first and last day** are included. For example, Monday to Friday is **5 days**.
- Leave is booked in **whole days**. Half days are not supported.

---

## 4. When can you take leave?

You can apply for leave at any time, as long as the request passes these checks:

- ✅ You have **enough balance** for that leave type (pending requests count against it too).
- ✅ The dates **do not overlap** another pending or approved leave of yours.
- ✅ The dates are within **one calendar year**. If your leave runs from December into January, submit **two separate requests**.
- ✅ The range contains **at least one working day** (a weekend-only request is rejected).

The app will tell you straight away if one of these fails.

**Good practice (⚠️ HR TO CONFIRM exact rules):**
- **Annual leave:** apply as early as possible, ideally at least ___ working days in advance.
- **Sick leave:** apply as soon as you are able, on the first day of absence if possible. A medical certificate may be required for absences longer than ___ days.
- **Casual leave:** for short-notice needs. Please tell your manager directly as well.
- Avoid booking leave during critical team deadlines without discussing it with your manager first.

---

## 5. How to apply

1. Go to **🏠 My Leave**.
2. In **✈️ Plan a break**, choose the **leave type**.
3. Pick the **From** and **To** dates.
4. Add a **reason** (optional but helpful).
5. Click **Submit request 🚀**.

Your request is now **⏳ Pending**, and the days are held against your balance until a decision is made.

---

## 6. What happens after you apply

| Status | Meaning | Your balance |
|---|---|---|
| ⏳ Pending | Waiting for a manager or admin to review | Days are held (reserved) |
| ✅ Approved | Your leave is confirmed and shows on the Team Calendar | Days are used |
| ❌ Rejected | Not approved. The reviewer may leave a comment explaining why | Days are returned |
| 🚫 Cancelled | You withdrew the request | Days are returned |

- You can see the reviewer's **comment** next to the status in **🗂️ My requests**.
- You can **cancel** a pending or approved request from **My requests** with the **Cancel** button. The days go back to your balance.
- Managers and admins **cannot approve their own** requests. Another manager or admin reviews them.

---

## 7. Paid vs unpaid leave: what you pay

- **Annual, Sick and Casual leave are paid.** There is no deduction from your salary for these days, as long as they are approved and within your balance.
- **Unpaid leave is not paid.** Your salary is reduced for each approved unpaid leave day.
- The app **does not calculate salary deductions**. Payroll does this from the approved leave records.

**Example only (⚠️ HR TO CONFIRM the actual formula):**

```
Daily rate       = monthly salary ÷ working days in that month
Deduction        = daily rate × approved unpaid leave days

e.g. salary 3,000, 20 working days in the month, 3 unpaid days
     daily rate = 3,000 ÷ 20 = 150
     deduction  = 150 × 3   = 450
```

If you run out of paid leave, the app will refuse a paid-type request that exceeds your balance. You can then apply for **Unpaid** leave instead (up to 30 days per year).

---

## 8. Checking balances and your team's leave

- **Balances:** shown on cards on **My Leave**, with a bar for used (coloured) and pending (striped) days.
- **Team Calendar:** shows approved leave for the whole team, so you can plan around colleagues' absences. Use the arrows to change month.

---

## 9. Quick answers

**I made a mistake in my dates.** Cancel the request and submit a new one.

**My request says "Insufficient balance".** You've used (or reserved) all days of that type. Check My Leave, or apply for Unpaid leave.

**It says I already have leave on those dates.** Another pending or approved request overlaps. Cancel it first or choose different dates.

**My leave spans two years.** Submit one request up to 31 December and another from 1 January.

**I forgot my password.** Ask your admin for help. (Self-service password reset is not available yet.)

**Who approves my leave?** Any manager or admin. Your direct manager should normally review it.

## 10. For admins: onboarding a new employee

1. Sign in as admin and open **👥 Users**.
2. Under **Welcome a new teammate**, enter the name, email, a temporary password (at least 6 characters), and the role.
3. Click **Create user ✨**, then send the employee their login details and this guide.
4. The employee automatically gets the standard yearly balances listed in section 2.
